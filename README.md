# Vrat Lens

Vrat Lens tells you whether you can eat something on a fasting day, based on the food rules of your tradition.

Take a photo of a plate, a menu or a packaged food label. Vrat Lens separates each item, identifies it, and checks it against a rules engine for your tradition (starting with Ekadashi and Jain rules). Each item gets a clear allowed / not allowed result with the reason, in Hindi or English.

## Built with
- SAM 3.1 for separating items on a plate
- Muse Spark for identifying dishes and reading ingredient lists
- A Muse Spark tool-calling agent over a structured rules engine
- Muse Image for allowed-alternative cards

## Status
